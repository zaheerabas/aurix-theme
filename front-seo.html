Abdul Raffay leads Aurix with a legacy rooted in nearly four generations of surgical and dental instrument craftsmanship dating to the late 1800s. Beginning on the factory floor at just 16, he mastered both craft and commerce — shaping Aurix into a clinician-focused brand built on precision, trust, and real-world usability.
